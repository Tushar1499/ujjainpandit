Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you browse this website or send a puja enquiry, and how to contact us about that information.

Information you submit

If you send an enquiry through a form, the website asks for your name, mobile number, email address and selected puja. The form also records which page the enquiry came from. The enquiry is sent to Pandit Ram Lakhan Sharma so he can respond about your request.

If you call or use WhatsApp, the information you choose to share is handled by your phone or messaging provider and by the person you contact.

Website measurement

This website uses Google tags and Google Tag Manager for website measurement and advertising conversion reporting. Some English pages also load TrafficGuard for traffic measurement. These tools may process technical information such as browser, device, page visit and interaction data. Their handling of data is also governed by their own privacy policies and settings.

How information is used

Contact details submitted in a form are used to respond to the puja enquiry and discuss the requested service. Technical and measurement information is used to understand visits, measure enquiries and support advertising.

Retention and requests

Enquiry messages may remain in the email account used to receive them and in service or hosting logs for as long as they are needed to respond, maintain the service or meet applicable requirements. To ask about, correct or request deletion of information you submitted, contact us using the details below.

Third-party services

Google, TrafficGuard, your internet or messaging provider, and the website hosting provider may handle information as part of their services. Please review the privacy information from the provider you use for details of its practices.
